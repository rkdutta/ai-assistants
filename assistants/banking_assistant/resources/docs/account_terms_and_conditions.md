# Account Terms and Conditions

**Document type:** Policy
**Applies to:** Checking, Savings, and Business accounts
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Account Opening

Accounts may be opened by any individual aged 18 or older, or by an authorized
signatory on behalf of a business, subject to identity verification (see the
KYC/AML Policy). A minimum opening deposit of $25 is required for checking
and savings accounts; business accounts require a minimum opening deposit of
$500.

## 2. Fees

- **Monthly maintenance fee:** $0 for checking accounts with a minimum daily
  balance of $1,500 or more; $12/month otherwise.
- **Savings account fee:** $0 (no monthly fee).
- **Business account fee:** $25/month, waived with a minimum daily balance of
  $10,000.
- **Excessive withdrawal fee:** $5 per withdrawal after the sixth withdrawal
  from a savings account within a statement cycle.

See the Fee Schedule document for a full list of transaction fees (wire
transfers, foreign transactions, etc.).

## 3. Overdraft Policy

Checking accounts are eligible for standard overdraft coverage on debit card
purchases, checks, and ACH transactions up to $500. Each overdraft item
incurs a $34 overdraft fee, capped at 4 fees ($136) per business day.
Customers may opt out of overdraft coverage at any time; opted-out
transactions are declined rather than covered.

Accounts that remain overdrawn for more than 60 consecutive days may be
placed into collections and are subject to closure per Section 5.

## 4. Interest

Savings accounts earn interest at the posted Annual Percentage Yield (APY),
compounded daily and credited monthly. Checking accounts do not earn
interest unless enrolled in an interest-bearing checking product.

## 5. Account Closure

An account may be closed:

- **By the customer**, at any time, provided the balance is zero or the
  remaining balance is withdrawn or transferred at closing.
- **By the bank**, with 30 days' written notice, for accounts with a
  negative balance older than 60 days, confirmed fraudulent activity, or
  failure to complete required identity verification.

Closed accounts are retained in records for 7 years for regulatory purposes
but are no longer accessible to the customer.

## 6. Account Freezes

An account may be temporarily frozen (deposits allowed, withdrawals blocked)
pending investigation of suspicious activity, a legal order (e.g. levy or
garnishment), or unresolved identity verification. Frozen accounts are
reviewed within 10 business days; see the Dispute Resolution Policy for the
customer appeal process.
