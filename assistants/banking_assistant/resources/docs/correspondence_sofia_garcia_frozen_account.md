# Correspondence Notes — Sofia Garcia, Frozen Checking Account (AC-100007)

**Document type:** Customer correspondence / case notes
**Customer:** Sofia Garcia (user id 5)
**Related account:** AC-100007, checking, status: frozen, balance $0.00
**Case owner:** Fraud & Account Review, M. Petrov

## 2026-07-02 — Freeze applied

Automated monitoring flagged three rapid outbound transfers on 2026-07-01
totaling $14,850, moved to two external accounts not previously associated
with the customer, immediately followed by a card-not-present purchase
attempt of $4,200 that was declined. Pattern is consistent with account
takeover. Account AC-100007 frozen per the KYC/AML Policy pending
investigation; customer's linked savings account (AC-100008) was not frozen
as no suspicious activity was observed there.

## 2026-07-03 — Customer contacted

Reached customer by phone using the phone number on file. Customer confirmed
she did not initiate the transfers and had received a phishing email on
2026-06-29 impersonating the bank, requesting login verification. Customer
believes she may have entered credentials on the linked site. Advised
customer to change her password and enable multi-factor authentication.
Opened a fraud case and initiated the dispute process per the Dispute
Resolution Policy.

## 2026-07-04 — Provisional credit issued

Provisional credit of $14,850.00 issued to a temporary holding sub-account
pending investigation completion, per standard 10-business-day provisional
credit timeline. Explained to customer that the account itself will remain
frozen until identity re-verification is complete, but funds are protected.

## 2026-07-09 — Identity re-verification completed

Customer completed in-branch identity re-verification with photo ID.
Fraud investigation confirmed the two receiving accounts are known to be
associated with a prior fraud ring flagged by the network. Recommended
account be unfrozen with a new account number issued, since the compromised
account number should be treated as exposed.

## 2026-07-11 — Resolution

New checking account issued to replace AC-100007; the provisional credit
was made permanent and transferred to the new account. AC-100007 remains
frozen/closed and will not be reused. Customer notified of new account
details by secure message and asked to update any autopay/direct deposit
arrangements accordingly.

**Status as of this note:** Case closed. AC-100007 remains permanently
frozen as a matter of record; no further customer action required.
