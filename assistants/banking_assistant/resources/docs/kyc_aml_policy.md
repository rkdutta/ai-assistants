# KYC / AML Policy

**Document type:** Compliance Policy
**Applies to:** All new and existing account holders

## 1. Customer Identification Program (CIP)

Before opening any account, the bank collects and verifies:

- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Residential address
- Government-issued identification number (SSN, ITIN, or passport number
  for non-residents)

Identity is verified against a government-issued photo ID and, where
available, a third-party identity verification service. Accounts may not be
funded until identity verification is complete.

## 2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

At account opening, the bank assesses the customer's expected activity
(anticipated transaction volume, source of funds, purpose of account) to
establish a baseline risk profile. Business accounts additionally require
beneficial ownership information for any individual owning 25% or more of
the entity.

## 3. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Enhanced review is required for:

- Customers flagged as Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
- Accounts with expected international wire activity exceeding $50,000/month
- Business accounts in cash-intensive industries (e.g. money services
  businesses, cannabis-adjacent businesses)

EDD includes more frequent transaction monitoring and periodic re-verification
of customer information (at least annually).

## 4. Suspicious Activity Monitoring

Transactions are monitored for patterns consistent with money laundering or
fraud, including:

- Structuring (multiple deposits just under the $10,000 reporting threshold)
- Rapid movement of funds in and out of an account with no clear business
  purpose
- Transactions inconsistent with the customer's stated profile or history

Flagged activity is reviewed by the compliance team within 5 business days.
Confirmed suspicious activity is documented in a Suspicious Activity Report
(SAR) and filed with the appropriate regulator; SAR filings and their
underlying details are confidential and must not be disclosed to the
customer.

## 5. Currency Transaction Reporting

Cash transactions (deposits or withdrawals) exceeding $10,000 in a single
business day, whether in one transaction or several related transactions,
are reported via a Currency Transaction Report (CTR) as required by law.

## 6. Record Retention

Customer identification records, account opening documentation, and
transaction monitoring records are retained for a minimum of 5 years after
account closure.
