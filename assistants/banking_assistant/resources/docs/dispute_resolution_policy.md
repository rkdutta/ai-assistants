# Dispute Resolution Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Applies to:** All account types, debit card transactions, and ACH transfers

## 1. Filing a Dispute

Customers may dispute a transaction (unauthorized charge, duplicate charge,
incorrect amount, goods/services not received) within 60 days of the
statement date on which the transaction appeared. Disputes are filed via the
customer support channel and require:

- The account number and transaction date/amount
- A brief description of the issue
- Any supporting documentation (receipts, correspondence with the merchant)

## 2. Provisional Credit

For unauthorized debit card transactions, a provisional credit equal to the
disputed amount is issued to the customer's account within 10 business days
of the dispute being filed, pending investigation. Provisional credit is not
issued for disputes involving authorized transactions where the disagreement
is with the merchant (e.g. product quality) rather than the bank.

## 3. Investigation Timeline

- **Standard disputes:** Resolved within 10 business days.
- **Complex disputes** (e.g. involving international merchants or
  point-of-sale system errors): May take up to 45 days. The customer is
  notified in writing if the investigation will exceed 10 business days.
- **New account disputes** (account open less than 30 days): May take up to
  90 days.

## 4. Outcomes

- **Dispute upheld:** Provisional credit (if issued) becomes permanent. If no
  provisional credit was issued, the amount is credited at resolution.
- **Dispute denied:** Provisional credit is reversed, and the customer is
  notified in writing with the reason for denial and the right to request
  supporting documentation used in the investigation.

## 5. Escalation

If a customer disagrees with the outcome, they may request a second review
by a senior dispute specialist within 30 days of the decision. If still
unresolved, the customer may file a complaint with the relevant banking
regulator; the bank will provide the appropriate regulator contact
information on request.

## 6. Frozen Account Appeals

Where a dispute or investigation results in an account freeze (see Account
Terms and Conditions, Section 6), the customer may request expedited review.
Expedited reviews are completed within 5 business days when the customer
demonstrates the freeze is causing financial hardship (e.g. inability to pay
rent or make payroll for a business account).
