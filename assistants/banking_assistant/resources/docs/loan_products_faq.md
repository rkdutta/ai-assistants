# Loan Products FAQ

**Document type:** Product FAQ
**Applies to:** Mortgage, Auto, Personal, and Student loans

## What loan types do you offer?

- **Mortgage:** 15- or 30-year fixed terms, rates typically 3.5%–5.5% APR
  depending on credit profile and down payment.
- **Auto:** 36–72 month terms, rates typically 5.5%–8.5% APR.
- **Personal:** 12–60 month terms, unsecured, rates typically 8%–15% APR.
- **Student:** 60–180 month terms, rates typically 4.5%–6.5% APR, with
  in-school deferment available.

## How is my interest rate determined?

Rates are risk-based, determined primarily by credit score, debt-to-income
ratio, and (for secured loans) loan-to-value ratio. Existing customers with
an active checking account in good standing may qualify for a 0.25% rate
discount on new loans.

## Can I repay my loan early?

Yes. All loan products may be repaid in full or in part at any time with no
prepayment penalty. Extra payments are applied to principal by default
unless the customer requests otherwise in writing.

## What happens if I miss a payment?

- **1–29 days late:** A late fee of the greater of $25 or 5% of the payment
  amount is applied. No adverse credit reporting.
- **30–89 days late:** Loan is reported to credit bureaus as delinquent.
  A collections specialist will attempt contact to arrange a repayment plan.
- **90+ days late:** Loan is classified as delinquent in our systems and may
  be referred to a collections agency. Secured loans (mortgage, auto) may
  enter the default/repossession process per the loan agreement.

## What hardship options are available?

Customers experiencing financial hardship (job loss, medical emergency,
natural disaster) may request:

- **Payment deferment:** Pause payments for up to 3 months; interest
  continues to accrue.
- **Loan modification:** Temporary or permanent adjustment to the payment
  schedule, term length, or interest rate, subject to underwriting review.
- **Forbearance plan:** A structured reduced-payment plan for up to 6
  months, documented in a signed agreement with the customer.

Hardship requests are handled case-by-case; see individual customer
correspondence records for examples of negotiated arrangements.

## Is mortgage insurance required?

Private mortgage insurance (PMI) is required on conventional mortgages with
a down payment below 20% of the home's appraised value. PMI is automatically
removed once the loan balance reaches 78% of the original appraised value.
