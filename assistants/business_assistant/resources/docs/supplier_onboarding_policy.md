# Supplier Onboarding Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Applies to:** All raw material, packaging, and logistics suppliers

## 1. Onboarding Requirements

New suppliers must provide:

- Business name, tax ID, and remittance address
- Standard lead times for their product category
- Delivery/order notice requirements (e.g. minimum notice before a delivery
  window)
- References or a trial order before being approved for recurring purchase
  orders

## 2. Standard Payment Terms

Default supplier payment terms are **net-45** from invoice/delivery date,
consistent with the terms currently in place with Highland Bean Co. Terms
may be negotiated down to net-30 for new or smaller suppliers, or extended
for high-volume strategic suppliers at renewal.

## 3. Delivery Notice Requirements

Suppliers with perishable or scheduling-sensitive goods (e.g. dairy) may
require advance notice for orders — Dairy Direct, for example, requires 48
hours' notice ahead of any order to guarantee weekly delivery. Order notice
requirements are recorded in each supplier's account notes and should be
checked before placing a purchase order.

## 4. Price Change Notification

Suppliers are expected to provide at least 30 days' written notice of any
price increase. Recurring price increases (e.g. quarterly, as with Bakewell
Ingredients) should be tracked so budgeting can account for them in advance
rather than being surprised at each cycle.

## 5. Performance Review

Supplier reliability (on-time delivery rate, order accuracy, responsiveness
to delay notifications) is reviewed annually. Suppliers with a pattern of
missed delivery windows (e.g. shipping delays) are flagged for a
performance conversation before contract renewal; persistent issues may
trigger sourcing a backup supplier for the affected category.
