# Billing Dispute and Credit Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Applies to:** Customer invoices and supplier purchase orders

## 1. Customer-Initiated Invoice Disputes

Customers may dispute an invoice (incorrect quantity, pricing error,
damaged/short shipment) within 15 days of the invoice date. Disputes should
be logged against the invoice with a brief description; the invoice is held
in "pending" status pending resolution rather than escalated to "overdue".

## 2. Resolution and Credit Memos

- **Confirmed billing error:** A credit memo is issued for the disputed
  amount and applied to the customer's next invoice, or refunded directly
  if the customer requests it.
- **Partial shipment/damage:** A prorated credit is issued for the affected
  portion of the order only.
- **Dispute not upheld:** The customer is notified with the reasoning
  (e.g. delivery confirmation, signed receipt) and the invoice reverts to
  its normal due date and late-fee schedule.

## 3. Supplier-Side Discrepancies

When a delivered purchase order doesn't match what was ordered (wrong
quantity, item substitution, quality issue), the discrepancy is logged
against the PO and the supplier is notified within 5 business days of
delivery. Payment for the disputed portion is withheld until resolved;
undisputed portions of the invoice are paid on the normal schedule.

## 4. Goodwill Credits

Account managers may issue a goodwill credit (typically capped at $100 or
5% of the order, whichever is greater) for service issues that don't
qualify as a billing error but affected the customer relationship — for
example, a late delivery that caused the customer inconvenience. Goodwill
credits should be noted in the customer's account file for context on
future interactions.
