**Customer:** Golden Hour Coffee Co (customer id 7)

## 2026-03-11 — First reminder email of the year

Invoice from February remained unpaid 22 days past due. Sent standard
reminder email per the Payment Terms Policy. Customer replied same day
apologizing, citing a change in their bookkeeping staff, and paid within 48
hours. No late fee applied given prompt response and otherwise reasonable
history at the time.

## 2026-06-25 — Second reminder email

Invoice #7 ($512.00, due 2026-07-20) followed up on 2026-06-25 after no
response to the automated reminder. Owner (contacted via team@
goldenhourcoffee.com) explained a slow month in sales and asked for a short
extension. Agreed to a 2-week extension to 2026-08-03 with no late fee, on
the condition that future invoices be paid on time going forward. Noted in
account file as the second reminder this year.

## 2026-08-05 — Extension missed, escalation

Extended due date of 2026-08-03 passed with no payment on invoice #7, and a
second invoice (#8, $488.75, issued 2026-07-25) is now also outstanding.
Combined overdue balance: $1,000.75. Escalated to account manager for a
direct call rather than another email reminder, per the repeat-late-payer
guidance in the Payment Terms Policy.

## 2026-08-12 — Call outcome

Spoke with the owner directly. Cash flow issue confirmed to be ongoing, not
a one-off. Owner committed to paying invoice #7 in full by 2026-08-20 and
invoice #8 by 2026-09-05, split into two payments to ease cash flow. Advised
that continued lateness may result in the account being moved to
shorter payment terms (net-15) or prepayment at next review, per policy.

**Status as of this note:** Both invoices remain in "overdue" status
pending the agreed payment dates. Flagged for follow-up if 2026-08-20
payment is missed.
