# Payment Terms Policy

**Document type:** Policy
**Applies to:** All wholesale customers

## 1. Standard Terms

Unless a customer-specific agreement states otherwise, invoices are issued
with **net-30** payment terms: full payment is due 30 days from the issue
date. Terms are noted per-customer during onboarding and recorded in the
customer's account notes (e.g. "pays net-30").

## 2. Late Payment

- **1–14 days late:** A friendly reminder email is sent automatically; no
  fee is applied.
- **15–29 days late:** A second reminder is sent, and the account is flagged
  for manual follow-up by the accounts team.
- **30+ days late:** Invoice is marked "overdue" in the system. A late fee
  of 1.5% of the invoice amount per month may be applied at the accounts
  team's discretion, taking into account the customer's payment history.

Repeat late payers (three or more late payments within a rolling 12-month
period) may be moved to prepayment or shortened payment terms (e.g. net-15)
at renewal.

## 3. Early Payment Discounts

Customers may be offered a 2% discount for payment within 10 days of
invoice issue ("2/10 net 30"), at the account manager's discretion,
typically extended to high-volume or long-tenured customers as a
relationship incentive rather than a standard offer.

## 4. Purchase Order Requirements

Some customers require a purchase order (PO) number to appear on every
invoice before they will process payment (see individual customer notes,
e.g. Evergreen Grocers). Invoices missing a required PO number are held
and not sent until the PO number is confirmed with the customer.

## 5. Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods are ACH transfer, check, and wire transfer for
invoices over $5,000. Card payments are not accepted for wholesale
invoices due to processing fee overhead.
